# Logistics Delivery Performance Analytics

An end-to-end data analytics project focused on analyzing logistics delivery performance, identifying high-risk shipping segments, understanding the root causes of delivery delays, and generating business recommendations.

The project combines **Python, PostgreSQL, SQL and Power BI** to transform logistics data into actionable business insights.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The objective of this project is to evaluate delivery performance across different shipping modes, regions, product categories and customer segments.

The analysis focuses on identifying:

- Delivery delays and late-delivery patterns
- High-risk shipping modes
- High-risk geographic regions
- Shipping gaps between scheduled and actual delivery
- Category and customer-segment performance
- Business impact of delivery delays
- Priority areas requiring operational improvement

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Measure overall logistics delivery performance
- Identify the shipping modes with the highest late-delivery rates
- Analyze regional delivery risks
- Identify product categories associated with higher delivery risk
- Compare actual shipping time with scheduled shipping time
- Quantify sales and profit associated with delivery performance
- Identify high-priority operational segments
- Develop actionable recommendations for improving delivery performance
